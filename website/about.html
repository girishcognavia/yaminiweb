Our story

A trusted name in air conditioning

Since 2009, Yamini Enterprises has been a leading name in Jodhpur for distributing high-quality air conditioners.

As Hitachi's channel partner, we offer a complete choice of VRF systems, ductable air conditioners, light commercial air conditioners, chillers, inverter split ACs, fixed speed split ACs and window ACs. Our customers value them for their performance and long service life.

Every space is different, so we offer air conditioners in a wide range of specifications and match each one to our customers' specific needs. From our Hitachi Studio showroom in Ratanada and our own warehouse, we serve homeowners, businesses, contractors and dealers alike.
