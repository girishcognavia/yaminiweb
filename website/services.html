FAQ

Common questions

Which AC capacity (tonnage) do I need?

It depends on room size, sun exposure, number of people and appliances. As a rough guide, small bedrooms often need about 1 ton and larger living rooms 1.5–2 tons, but we recommend a free site survey so we can size it correctly.

Inverter or fixed speed: which should I buy?

Inverter ACs adjust their speed to the cooling needed, so they use less electricity and keep the temperature steadier. They cost more upfront but usually save money if you run the AC for many hours a day. Fixed speed models suit lighter or occasional use.
